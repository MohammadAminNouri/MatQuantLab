# MatQuantLab

## Material-driven industrial risk research

MatQuantLab studies how material, energy, macro and geopolitical shocks transmit into manufacturing-linked assets.

The purpose is not to predict stock prices directly. The purpose is to understand:

```text
material shock → industrial exposure → market response
```

## Main workflow

1. **Brief** — a plain-language summary of the current stress state.
2. **Stress Monitor** — materials, energy, precious metals, macro and AM/powder-metallurgy stress.
3. **Exposure Map** — how shocks transmit into assets and manufacturing companies.
4. **Signals** — model-supported positive/negative pressure with trust level and drivers.
5. **News & Events** — GDELT, Google News RSS and configured RSS feeds, including GCIRD if reachable.
6. **Data Quality** — downloaded tickers, feature construction, period actually used and source status.

## What makes it different

MatQuantLab is built around materials logic:

- copper, aluminum, nickel, steel chain, coal, uranium, oil, gas, gold and silver
- rare earths and battery materials
- aerospace and defense material pathways
- additive manufacturing and powder metallurgy
- LPBF / EBM / DED / HIP / metal powder feedstock stress
- manufacturing and listed-company exposure
- contradictions between material stress, news and market signals

## Recommended settings

Long-term research:

```text
Data interval: 1d
History: 5y or 10y
Model selection: Adaptive
Research horizon: 5–20 bars
Shock window: 20
```

Short-term monitoring:

```text
Data interval: 5m or 15m
History: 5d or 1mo
Model selection: Adaptive
Research horizon: 3–12 bars
Shock window: 5–20
```

## Disclaimer

This is a research workbench, not investment advice.  
Market data from free public sources may be delayed, incomplete or unavailable for some tickers.
