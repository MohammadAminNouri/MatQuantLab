# Limitations and ethics

This project is for education and research.

## Limitations

- Yahoo Finance data can contain errors.
- Some delisted companies are missing, creating survivorship bias.
- Free data may not have institutional timestamp accuracy.
- Macro data can be revised after the original release.
- Backtests are simplified and do not model full execution constraints.
- Strong historical performance can still be overfitting.

## Ethical use

Do not present the project as an investment product. Do not claim guaranteed returns. Do not use it to advise others financially.
