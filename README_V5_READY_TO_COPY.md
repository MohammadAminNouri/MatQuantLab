# MatQuantLab V5 — Pro Critical Materials Intelligence

MatQuantLab is not a stock-prediction toy.

It is a **materials-informed industrial stress radar** that links commodities, macro stress, global indices, geopolitical news, and exposure scoring to generate interpretable early-warning research signals for industrial assets.

## What To Look At In The App

### GCMSI
Global Critical Materials Stress Index.

- High GCMSI: global material/macro stress is rising.
- High GCMSI + high VIX: risk-off industrial stress.
- High energy stress: potential margin pressure for industrial/aerospace sectors.
- High critical minerals + high geopolitical risk: supply-chain policy/export-control risk.

### Prediction Table
The table gives:

- asset
- signal direction
- intensity
- prediction value
- confidence
- validation correlation
- RMSE
- top 3 drivers
- suggested research action

This is not a trading signal. It is a research pressure indicator.
